# Ruff Family Feud 🦃

Family Feud for Thanksgiving. The TV shows the board, phones are the buzzers, and the family survey becomes the answers.

- `/#tv`: open on the TV and press Start
- `/#host`: the host's phone (PIN protected)
- `/#join`: players scan the QR code on the TV
- `/#survey`: the family survey link
- `/#admin`: questions and survey results (PIN protected)

Plain HTML + JavaScript, no build step. Data lives in Supabase (run `supabase-setup.sql` once).
